# Workflow Catalog

Workflows are the primary operational view of the domain. They cross domain-area boundaries and describe business activity independently of whether each step is manual, external, or eventually supported by the ERP.

## Standard workflow form

Each workflow uses the same sections:

- purpose and completion condition;
- participants;
- triggering information and prerequisites;
- normal flow;
- meaningful variations and exceptions;
- records produced or changed;
- connections to other workflows and systems; and
- decisions still requiring validation.

Detailed step order should be treated as proposed until reviewed with the people who perform the work.
