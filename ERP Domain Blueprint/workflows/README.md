# Workflow Catalog

Workflows are the primary operational view of the domain. They cross domain-area boundaries and describe business activity independently of whether each step is manual, external, or eventually supported by the ERP.

## Initial workflows

The following are discovery candidates, not accepted company procedures:

### Establish a project and its commercial basis

Begins when a client need or opportunity is accepted for formal handling. Ends
when the project, responsible parties, site, governing agreement, commercial
baseline, and initial authority are known—or when the opportunity is closed.

### Establish and control the project baseline

Begins with understood scope. Ends when a versioned budget, APU/resource basis,
schedule or cash-flow expectation, and approval state are known. Approved
changes should remain distinguishable from the original baseline.

### Purchase to project cost and payment

Begins with a need for a good or service. Ends when receipt, supplier obligation,
project or overhead allocation, settlement, tax treatment, and accounting
consequence are explainable.

This is the recommended first detailed walkthrough because direct accounting
evidence exists, but the client may select another workflow if it is more
foundational.

### Contractor work to claim and payment

Begins with an authorized contractor relationship or work assignment. Ends when
measured work, review, deductions, approval, payment, and cost consequence are
explained.

### Workforce activity to payroll obligation and payment

Begins with an accepted worker relationship and compensable activity or period.
Ends when the amount, approval, settlement, and project-cost consequence are
explained.

### Client progress to invoice and collection

Begins with work or a milestone eligible for certification. Ends when the
client-facing amount, approval, invoice, collection, retainage, and outstanding
balance are explained.

### Project change/addition

Begins when scope, quantity, cost, price, or time may change. Ends when the
proposal is approved, rejected, or withdrawn and its effect on baseline, cash
flow, delivery, and billing is explicit.

### Project closeout

Begins when delivery approaches completion. Ends when outstanding work,
documents, balances, retainage, handover, warranty or follow-up, and final
project status are resolved.

## Standard workflow form

Each workflow uses the same sections:

- purpose and completion condition;
- participants;
- triggering information and prerequisites;
- normal flow;
- meaningful variations and exceptions;
- records produced or changed;
- connections to other workflows and systems; and
- decisions still requiring validation.

Detailed step order should be treated as proposed until reviewed with the people who perform the work.
