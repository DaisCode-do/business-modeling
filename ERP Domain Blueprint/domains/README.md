# Domain Documentation

Create one domain page only after the client recognizes the area as a useful
business boundary. The proposed areas are listed in the
[Domain Map](../foundation/DOMAIN-MAP.md); they are not yet final.

## Standard domain form

Each domain page should contain:

1. **Purpose and boundary** — the business responsibility it explains and what
   it deliberately does not own.
2. **Language** — the concepts whose meanings belong to this area.
3. **Responsibilities** — decisions and records maintained by the area.
4. **Relationships** — important connections within and across areas.
5. **Invariants to validate** — statements that must remain true if adopted.
6. **Workflows** — links to end-to-end activity that crosses the area.
7. **External systems** — authorities, working tools, evidence, references, and
   transition sources.
8. **Open questions** — policy or meaning still preventing a consistent model.

Domain pages describe business meaning. They do not allocate microservices,
database schemas, teams, or menus.
