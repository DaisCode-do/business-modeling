# Finance, Accounting, and Tax

**Status:** Placeholder pending workflow validation

## Proposed purpose

Explain why money is owed, what money moved, how it was applied, how it agrees
with external evidence, and what accounting or tax consequence follows.

The prior evidence suggests useful distinctions among obligation, invoice or
claim, payment, allocation, bank evidence, reconciliation, tax snapshot, and
accounting entry. These remain hypotheses until real workflows and exceptions
are reviewed with the accountant and the relevant operational owner.

## First workflows to validate

- supplier invoice to project cost, payment, and DGII reporting;
- contractor claim approval to payment and accounting consequence;
- client progress certification to invoice, collection, and receipt; and
- payroll or worker obligation to settlement.

## Boundary questions

- Which records are operational finance, statutory accounting, tax reporting,
  or external-accountant responsibility?
- What creates an obligation in each workflow?
- What evidence proves money movement and who confirms its business purpose?
- Which corrections require reversal, replacement, credit, or another controlled
  record?
- Which amounts and classifications become immutable after approval, posting, or
  submission?
