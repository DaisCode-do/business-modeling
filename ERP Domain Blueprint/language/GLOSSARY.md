# Domain Glossary

This glossary provides the default meaning of terms used across the blueprint. Definitions are intentionally independent of screens or database structures.

## Seed terms for validation

These definitions are proposed starting points. They should be corrected during
workflow reviews rather than approved as an isolated vocabulary exercise.

### Party

A person or organization known to the business. Supplier, contractor, client,
worker, owner, and contact are candidate roles or relationships rather than
automatically separate identities.

### Project

A controlled construction undertaking with an identity, responsible parties,
location or sites, lifecycle, contractual context, plans, costs, delivery
history, and client-facing consequences. The exact boundary of one project is
open.

### Contract or Agreement

Accepted terms among parties that govern scope, responsibility, value, timing,
approval, or another business relationship for an effective period. The company
must confirm when it uses each term and which evidence establishes acceptance.

### Cost Center

A business destination used to explain responsibility for cost. A project may be
a cost context, while overhead or temporarily unallocated cost may require a
non-project cost center.

### Budget Baseline

The approved version against which project cost, scope, progress, forecast, and
change can be compared. Whether the company currently maintains such a baseline
and who approves it remain open.

### APU

The company's meaning of unit-price analysis, including the resources,
quantities, rates, assumptions, and effective version used to explain a unit
price. The exact term and approval policy require validation.

### Purchase Invoice

A supplier document or accepted record that may establish an obligation for
goods or services after required review. It does not by itself prove payment.

### Contractor Claim

A contractor-facing request or certification of measured work and resulting
amount. It must remain distinguishable from client progress certification and
payroll support even if current practice calls each one `cubicacion`.

### Client Progress Certification

A client-facing record of work or progress accepted for commercial billing or
contract administration. Its local business name remains open.

### Obligation

An explainable amount owed by or to the company because of an accepted business
cause.

### Payment

A recorded movement of money. Payment is separate from the invoice, claim,
payroll amount, or receivable that explains why money is owed.

### Payment Allocation

The application of all or part of a payment to one or more obligations.

### Evidence

Information used to support observation, review, approval, reconciliation, or
decision. Evidence is not automatically an authoritative business record.

## Terms requiring early clarification

- `cubicacion` across contractor, client, and workforce contexts;
- project, job, site, contract, cost center, and source project code;
- employee, worker, day laborer, crew member, and contractor;
- addition, change order, extra work, and budget revision;
- invoice, receipt, payment proof, and settlement; and
- approved, posted, submitted, voided, corrected, and reversed.
